Cataloc — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

The short version

Cataloc does not collect anything. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK that reports on you. Everything you put into the app stays on your device.

The one time data leaves your phone is when you choose to sync a household with someone else's phone on your own Wi-Fi. That connection is directly between the two phones. It does not touch the internet, and the developer has no part in it and no access to it.

What Cataloc stores, and where

Cataloc stores the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • The households, containers and items you create, including their names, tags, quantities, best-before dates and loan details.
  • The photos you take or choose, saved as files inside the app's own folder.
  • A history of changes you have made within the app.
  • Your settings: language, appearance, and which household you last opened.

None of this is transmitted anywhere by default. It is not backed up to any service run by the developer, because no such service exists.

Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup on iOS, or Google's backup on Android) may include this data if you have those features enabled. That is between you and Apple or Google, and is governed by their privacy policies.

Syncing with another phone

Syncing is entirely opt-in and happens only while you are on the invite or join screen.

  • One phone shares a household and displays a single-use code, valid for five minutes. The other phone must present that code to connect.
  • The two phones then exchange that household directly over your local network: its containers, items, tags, history, member names, and the photos attached to them.
  • Nothing is sent to the internet. There is no server in the middle, no relay, and no copy retained anywhere by the developer.
  • The connection is open only while that screen is on screen. Cataloc does not listen on your network in the background and does not advertise itself.
  • Each phone keeps its own complete copy. Removing a member on one phone does not delete the copy already on the other — it only stops future syncs.

Be aware of what sharing means: once you sync a household with someone, they hold a full copy of it, exactly as if you had handed them an export file.

Device name. Each install has a name — taken from your device's own name unless you change it — and a random identifier. These are shared only with phones you have paired with, so that changes can be attributed to a person in the household's history. They are never sent anywhere else.

Permissions

Camera — requested only when you tap "Take photo". The image is written to the app's storage and is not uploaded. Declining the camera does not block anything: you can still pick pictures from your library, and items do not require a photo at all.

Photo library — requested only when you tap "Library". Cataloc reads only the single picture you select and stores a copy in its own folder.

Local network (iOS). Requested the first time you sync, so the two phones can find each other on your Wi-Fi. Used only for that, and only while a sync screen is open.

Cataloc requests no other permissions. It does not use location, contacts or the microphone, and it never sends your data to the internet.

In-app purchases

Cataloc is free for one household and 100 containers and items. Cataloc PRO is an optional subscription that removes both limits.

If you subscribe, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple or Google, using the payment method already on your Apple or Google account. Cataloc never sees your payment details, and there is no Cataloc account: renewals, price changes, refunds and cancellation are all handled by the store, and cancelling is done there rather than here. The developer receives the payout and the store's standard sales reporting, which contains no personal data.

Cataloc records only a local flag noting whether the store currently reports an active subscription, so the app works correctly when it is offline.

Nothing you have already catalogued is ever removed, hidden or made read-only, whether you subscribe, never subscribe, or subscribe and then stop.

Sharing and export

"Export everything" writes a single JSON file containing your data and photos, and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where that file then goes is entirely your choice. Cataloc does not upload it anywhere.

Printing or sharing a label produces a PDF through your device's own print and share systems.

Deleting your data

"Delete all data" on the Me tab erases every household, container, item, photo and history entry from the device permanently. Deleting the app does the same.

Because there is no server, there is no copy for the developer to hold, and therefore nothing to request the deletion of. Export first if you want to keep a copy — once it is gone, it is gone.

Children

Cataloc is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app and the date above will change.
