What's inside

Everything about every cut

From the butcher to the table, Mapa do Boi answers the questions of whoever buys and whoever cooks.

Map

The beef map

Cuts drawn on a Nelore steer. Tap a piece to see its name, where it sits on the animal, its tenderness and fat, and the other names it goes by.

Equivalents

One cut, many countries

Brazilian, Argentine and US cut charts, with the equivalents between them. Know what to ask your butcher when the recipe comes from abroad, or when you travel.

  • Brazil
  • Argentina
  • United States

Filters

Find the right cut

Filter by tenderness, fat and cooking method, and the map shows only the pieces that fit what you want to make.

Scanner

Scan the label

Take a photo of the butcher or supermarket label and the app finds the cut. Reading happens on your device: no image is sent.

Recipes

A recipe for every cut

From the grill to the oven, step-by-step recipes with time, temperature and doneness, tied to the right cut.

Timer

Timer on the Lock Screen

Start cooking and follow each step's timer right on the Lock Screen, without opening the app.

Search

Search by any name

Ribeye, bife ancho, entrecôte, contrafilé: type the name you know, in any language, and the app finds the cut.

Free

No ads. No account.

Mapa do Boi is free and complete. If it helps your barbecue, you can leave an optional tip inside the app.
