Perguntas frequentes

E se alguém clicar várias vezes de propósito?

Cada aparelho conta no máximo 10 cliques e 30 exibições por oferta a cada hora; o que passar disso não é cobrado. Se encontrar algo estranho no extrato, escreva para nós e estornamos os eventos.

Posso escolher em quais cortes apareço?

Sim. Cada produto é cadastrado em um corte e aparece na página desse corte. Se quiser, ele também aparece no mesmo corte nos mapas de outros países (Picanha, Tapa de cuadril, Top sirloin cap), onde a correspondência é exata.

Vendo só on-line. Posso anunciar?

Pode. Em vez de uma loja com endereço, você informa as áreas onde entrega, e a oferta aparece para quem está dentro delas.

E se o leitor achar que o anúncio está errado?

O leitor pode avisar que o produto não tinha a ver com o corte ou que não estava na loja. Os avisos aparecem para você no portal, e não custam nada. Um produto com muitos avisos sem resposta pode ser suspenso.

For butchers, grocers and stores

Advertise on Mapa do Boi

People who open a cut in the app are deciding what to buy. Your prices show right there, under “Where to buy”, to people near your store or in your delivery area.

Start advertising

How it shows

Under each cut, the three nearest offers, marked as sponsored. The rest are under “See all”, sorted by distance or by price per 100 g. A tap opens the product with its photo, price and address, and takes the reader to your site or to directions to your store.

Each product shows only on the cut it was filed under, only where it’s in stock, and only to people within the store’s reach or delivery area.

Pricing

No monthly fee, no contract and no monthly minimum. You add credit and pay only for what happened:

ImpressionR$ 0.01

When the offer is on screen. Counted once per device per hour.

ClickR$ 0.30

When the reader taps through to your site or to directions to your store.

Everything elseFree

Opening the product card, adding stores and products, the portal and the API.

Example: a week with 2,000 impressions and 60 clicks.

2,000 impressions × R$ 0.01R$ 20.00
60 clicks × R$ 0.30R$ 18.00
TotalR$ 38.00

When your balance runs out, your offers leave the app until the next top-up, so you never spend more than you put in. Every impression and click is recorded at the price of the moment, and you can check it all in the portal.

Getting started

  1. Get in touchEmail support@wiltonpelicari.com with your store’s name and city. We’ll set up your portal access.
  2. Add your productsEnter your stores or delivery areas, and products with photo, weight and price. In the portal, or through the API from your own system.
  3. Top upRequest a top-up in the portal. Once the payment is confirmed, the credit is added and your offers go live.

FAQ

What if someone clicks over and over on purpose?

Each device counts at most 10 clicks and 30 impressions per offer an hour; anything beyond that isn’t charged. If something looks off in your statement, write to us and we’ll refund those events.

Can I choose which cuts I show on?

Yes. Each product is filed under one cut and shows on that cut’s page. If you like, it also shows on the same cut in other countries’ charts (Picanha, Tapa de cuadril, Top sirloin cap), where the match is exact.

I only sell online. Can I advertise?

Yes. Instead of a store address, you enter the areas you deliver to, and the offer shows to people inside them.

What if a reader thinks the ad is wrong?

Readers can report that a product didn’t belong on that cut or wasn’t at the store. Reports show up for you in the portal and cost nothing. A product with many unanswered reports may be suspended.

Questions fréquentes

Et si quelqu’un clique encore et encore exprès ?

Chaque appareil compte au plus 10 clics et 30 affichages par offre et par heure ; au-delà, rien n’est facturé. Si quelque chose vous semble anormal dans votre relevé, écrivez-nous et nous rembourserons ces événements.

Puis-je choisir les pièces où j’apparais ?

Oui. Chaque produit est classé sous une pièce et s’affiche sur la page de cette pièce. Si vous le souhaitez, il s’affiche aussi sur la même pièce dans les chartes d’autres pays (Picanha, Tapa de cuadril, Top sirloin cap), là où la correspondance est exacte.

Je vends seulement en ligne. Puis-je annoncer ?

Oui. Au lieu d’une adresse, vous indiquez les zones où vous livrez, et l’offre s’affiche pour les gens qui s’y trouvent.

Et si quelqu’un trouve que l’annonce est fausse ?

Les gens peuvent signaler qu’un produit n’avait rien à voir avec la pièce ou n’était pas en boutique. Les signalements apparaissent dans votre portail et ne coûtent rien. Un produit avec beaucoup de signalements sans réponse peut être suspendu.
