T-bone

Bife angosto (no. 13) + Lomo (no. 14) · Argentine

A loin steak cut through the T-shaped bone: strip loin on one side, a small piece of tenderloin on the other. Two textures and plenty of flavor. On the grill, keep the tenderloin away from the hottest spot.
