Tomahawk

Filé de costela (no. 8) · Brazilian

A thick prime rib steak with the whole rib bone left on and scraped clean, about 30 cm long. The meat is the same as a ribeye, marbled and juicy. Its thickness calls for heat control: a hard sear, then indirect heat.
